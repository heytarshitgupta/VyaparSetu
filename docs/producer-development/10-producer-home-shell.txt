VYAPARSETU — PRODUCER DEVELOPMENT: RESPONSIVE APP SHELL & PRODUCER HOME
==============================================================================
Milestone: Step 6A (Post-Onboarding Experience)
Scope: Responsive Producer App Shell + Producer Home Screen
Owner: Producer Development Team
Dependencies: Flutter gen-l10n (English/Hindi/Punjabi), ThemeProvider, Supabase Auth

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. RESPONSIVE NAVIGATION ARCHITECTURE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Navigation across devices uses standard Material adaptive layout paradigms
governed by LayoutBuilder breakpoints rather than brittle device checks:

- Phone (< 640px width):
  * Material 3 bottom NavigationBar.
  * 4 primary destinations with icons and plain-language localized labels.
  * Large, comfortable touch targets (56dp+ height).
  * Safe area padding with bottom inset handling.

- Tablet (640px - 1024px width):
  * Left NavigationRail with icon + always-visible label.
  * Min-width 72dp with a prominent Add Product icon button in the header.
  * Allows comfortable side-by-side content consumption on tablets.

- Laptop / Desktop (> 1024px width):
  * Expanded permanent left navigation sidebar (width 250dp).
  * NOT an enterprise admin dashboard; simple, welcoming artisan portal.
  * Brand header with VyaparSetu icon and title.
  * Prominent [ + Add Product ] hero button immediately below the header.
  * 4 destination items featuring selected container treatment (background pill,
    distinct accent border, bold weight, and high contrast).
  * Centered content canvas constrained to maxWidth 1100dp to avoid stretched cards.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. FOUR PRIMARY DESTINATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The shell strictly maintains exactly four permanent navigation destinations:
  1. Home (Icons.home_outlined / Icons.home)
  2. My Products (Icons.inventory_2_outlined / Icons.inventory_2)
  3. Buyer Needs (Icons.handshake_outlined / Icons.handshake)
  4. My Profile (Icons.person_outline / Icons.person)

Critical Architectural Boundaries:
- Add Product is an ACTION, not a permanent navigation tab.
- What Buyers Want is a prominent HOME FEATURE / CARD, not a navigation tab.
- State is preserved across tab transitions using IndexedStack.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PRODUCER HOME VISUAL HIERARCHY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Home view is structured for maximum visual clarity for grassroots producers:

A. Friendly Identity Area:
   - Welcomes the producer by name (e.g., "Welcome, Ramesh Kumar" / "नमस्ते, ...")
   - Displays enterprise/business name and craft category subtly when available.
   - Zero exposure of internal database flags or verification jargon.

B. One Dominant Primary Action:
   - Visually dominant hero card for "+ Add Product" with rich terracotta accent.
   - Action icon + title + clear explanation: "Show buyers what you make".
   - Tapping routes to the Add Product action flow.

C. Important Shortcuts:
   - 📦 My Products ("See the products you have added") -> switches to Tab 1.
   - 📋 Buyer Needs ("See what buyers are looking for") -> switches to Tab 2.
   - 📈 What Buyers Want ("See what products people want") -> opens market preview.

D. Supporting Information (Truthful, Non-Fabricated States):
   - Useful empty states ("No products added yet", "Buyer needs will appear here").
   - ZERO fabricated dashboard analytics, fake earnings, fake buyer counts, or fake graphs.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. LOW-DIGITAL-LITERACY DESIGN PRINCIPLES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Target users include rural artisans, grassroots craftspeople, and micro-producers:
- Plain everyday phrasing with zero corporate/startup jargon.
- Every important action pairs a clear icon with a human-readable text label.
- Minimum 48x48dp interactive touch targets across all platforms.
- Obvious selected visual states (container highlight, borders, icons).
- Generous spacing and text scaling support.
- Responsive layout tested down to 320px narrow mobile viewports with zero overflows.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THEME-SAFE & MULTILINGUAL FOUNDATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Strict adherence to Theme.of(context).colorScheme and AppTheme tokens.
- Zero hardcoded hex colors or raw black/white backgrounds.
- High-contrast compliance in Light, Dark, and System appearance modes.
- 100% localized from day one via Flutter ARB in English, Hindi, and Punjabi.
- Instant, flicker-free language and appearance toggling without tab reset.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. VOICE-READY ARCHITECTURE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Action methods (selectDestination, openAddProduct, openWhatBuyersWant) are
  decoupled on the shell controller.
- Taps and future voice intents invoke the exact same methods.
- Microphone permissions, speech recognition, and AI models remain deferred.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. WHAT BUYERS WANT / MARKET INTELLIGENCE INTEGRATION (STEP BI-2)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Feature Ownership & Distinction:
   - BUYER NEEDS: Active real buyer requirements / matching posted on marketplace.
   - WHAT BUYERS WANT: Market Radar / Regional Demand Trends & Business Intelligence.
   - Reached via Producer Home shortcut: Home -> "What Buyers Want" -> WhatBuyersWantScreen.
   - The 4-destination navigation architecture (Home, My Products, Buyer Needs, Profile)
     remains strictly preserved.

B. Data Truthfulness & Static Sample Intelligence:
   - Current intelligence is STATIC / SAMPLE / PRECOMPUTED DEMO INTELLIGENCE.
   - NOT live buyer demand, NOT live sales analytics, and NOT real-time ML inference.
   - Technical implementation artifacts ("CSV-backed", filenames) are eliminated from UI.
   - UI prominently displays truthful sample indicators: "Sample market insights" badge
     and clear explanatory subtitle.

C. Reused Foundation:
   - Leverages teammate's existing ProducerMarketIntelligenceService, ProducerMarketSignal,
     and ProducerMarketIntelligenceDashboard without rewriting domain models.
   - Deterministic demand level thresholds:
     * High Demand: score >= 75
     * Medium Demand: score >= 50
     * Low Demand: score < 50

D. Responsive & Theme-Safe Presentation:
   - Fully responsive across Mobile (<640px), Tablet (640-1024px), and Desktop (>1024px).
   - Zero RenderFlex overflows down to 320px narrow mobile viewports.
   - Clean Light & Dark theme compatibility utilizing Theme.of(context).colorScheme.
   - 100% localized via Flutter gen-l10n across English, Hindi, and Punjabi, including
     canonical product category mapping (Agriculture, Textile, Food Processing, etc.).

E. Runtime Non-Use of CSVs & Future Live Limitations:
   - CSV datasets (products.csv, customers.csv, sales.csv) remain in assets/data for
     reference but are NOT read at runtime. Seed signals are static in-memory objects.
   - Future transition to live regional market intelligence will require backend DB
     aggregation tables / RPCs and should NOT attempt client-side runtime CSV parsing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. MY PROFILE, SETTINGS & SECURITY ARCHITECTURE (STEPS 6E & 6E.1)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Profile Header & Business Identity:
   - Clean initials circle avatar (derived from full name or "P").
   - Producer full name, business name, craft category, contact phone, and workshop address.
   - Fallbacks to localized "Not provided" for optional missing profile details.

B. Truthful Identity & Compliance Presentation:
   - Generic Truthful Identity Status: Displays "Identity Verification: Completed" or
     "Identity Verification: Pending" using genuine PAN verification and onboarding state.
   - Aadhaar Presentation Data Removed: Zero Aadhaar numbers, zero Aadhaar last-4,
     zero Aadhaar reference IDs, zero fingerprints, and zero fake verification statuses.
   - Safe GST Status: Simple, truthful compliance state ("GST: Registered" or
     "GST: Not Registered"). No premature GSTIN exposure or fake masking (•••• F1Z5).
   - Strict PAN Masking: Only last 4 digits are revealed (•••• 1234); raw PAN is never
     persisted in profile models or displayed in the UI.

C. Settings (Centralized Preferences):
   - App Language: English, Hindi (हिन्दी), Punjabi (ਪੰਜਾਬੀ) with reactive switching.
   - Voice Guidance Language: Follows app language or independent English/Hindi/Punjabi.
   - Appearance: System, Light, and Dark modes synced with ThemeProvider.

D. Account & Security:
   - Registered email presentation.
   - Real Supabase password-reset flow with status feedback.
   - Truthful Session Presentation: "Current Session: Signed in" without implying
     unimplemented multi-device tracking or hardware fingerprints.
   - Sign Out: Clear confirmation dialog routing cleanly back to AuthScreen.

E. Truthful Privacy Claims (Help & About):
   - Unsupported claims ("documents are encrypted", "government verified") eliminated.
   - Truthful representation: sensitive identity data is minimized, raw PAN is never
     rendered, Aadhaar is not carried in Profile, and producer data is guarded by
     authentication and database ownership policies.

F. Responsive Quick Action Menu:
   - Accessible from mobile/tablet AppBar and desktop sidebar footer.
   - Quick routes to My Profile, App Language, Appearance, Help & About, and Sign Out.
