1. ONBOARDING STEP & DATA MODEL ALIGNMENT
------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Basic Details
- Display Name, phone/email profile copy, primary language.

Step 2: Craft & Business Details
- Craft specializations (craft_category), workshop name (business_name), bio/experience.

Step 3: Location Details
- State (state), District (district), City (city), Pincode (pincode), Address (address), address_verification_status.

Step 4: Identity & Compliance
- PAN & Aadhaar: Handled via trusted verification workflow; client inputs are submitted to verification endpoints, which derive pan_last4, pan_hash, aadhaar_last4, and aadhaar_verification_reference.
- GST: Producer-declared gst_registered (boolean) and gstin (15-character identifier).

Step 5: Review & Submit
- Comprehensive summary review before finalizing onboarding and invoking a trusted RPC to transition onboarding_status to 'completed'.


2. SECURITY & TRUST BOUNDARIES
------------------------------------------------------------
- Client-Writable Fields: business_name, craft_category, bio, state, district, city, pincode, address, gst_registered, gstin.
- Trusted-Backend-Only Fields: onboarding_status, pan_last4, pan_hash, pan_verification_status, aadhaar_last4, aadhaar_verification_reference, aadhaar_verification_status, gst_verification_status, address_verification_status, verification_status.
- Direct Mutation Prohibited: Migration 005 explicitly revokes client INSERT/UPDATE privileges on onboarding_status.
- Prohibited from Storage: Raw 12-digit Aadhaar numbers and plaintext PAN numbers.
