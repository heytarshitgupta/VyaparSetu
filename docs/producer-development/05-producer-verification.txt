STATUS
------------------------------------------------------------
DATA MODEL EXPANDED & HARDENED (Step 4B0.2):
- Granular verification fields and trusted artifact columns defined in Migration 005.
- Client write privileges revoked on all verification artifact and status columns.
- Live API verification workflows remain PLANNED for subsequent stages.


1. VERIFICATION STATUS ARCHITECTURE
------------------------------------------------------------
A. Granular Domain Verifications:
- PAN Verification (pan_verification_status: unverified, pending, verified, rejected)
- Aadhaar / Identity Verification (aadhaar_verification_status: unverified, pending, verified, rejected)
- GST & Tax Compliance (gst_verification_status: not_applicable, unverified, pending, verified, rejected)
- Workshop Address (address_verification_status: unverified, pending, verified, rejected)

B. Overall Producer Status (producer_profiles.verification_status):
- Tracks overall merchant badge ('unverified', 'pending', 'verified', 'rejected').
- Overall status is derived on backend after individual checks; client cannot mutate directly.


2. TRUST BOUNDARIES & DATA MINIMIZATION
------------------------------------------------------------
- Raw Aadhaar Prohibited: Zero 12-digit Aadhaar numbers are accepted or stored.
- Trusted Identity Artifacts (Backend-Only):
  * aadhaar_last4 and aadhaar_verification_reference (token/reference returned by future verification service)
  * pan_last4 and pan_hash (backend-generated protected fingerprint or reference token; plaintext PAN is NEVER stored)
  * These columns are NOT client-writable and cannot be supplied directly by the Flutter app.
- GST Compliance Declaration:
  * gst_registered: Producer-supplied boolean indicating whether they are registered for GST.
  * gstin: Producer-supplied 15-character GSTIN.
  * gst_registered = FALSE means solely that the Producer declared they are not GST registered (legal threshold validation is outside prototype scope).
  * gst_verification_status defaults to 'not_applicable' when gst_registered = FALSE.
- Protected Verification Outcomes & Onboarding Status:
  * pan_verification_status, aadhaar_verification_status, gst_verification_status, address_verification_status, verification_status, and onboarding_status are strictly omitted from client write grants and can only be updated by trusted backend/admin logic.
